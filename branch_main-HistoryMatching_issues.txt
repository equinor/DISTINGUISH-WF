ISSUE 1
NO LOCALIZATION
====================================================================

DESCRIPTION

Current workflow:

    Local EM measurement
              ↓
         PET update
              ↓
       60 latent variables
              ↓
    Entire realization changes

The GeoSphere measurement contains information mainly around
the borehole and within its DOI.

However, the latent variables control the entire realization.

Potential consequence:

    A local observation may modify geological regions far away
    from the well.

This is currently considered the most likely source of
non-physical geological updates.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POTENTIAL SYMPTOMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

    Pink patches
    Reservoir bodies appearing far from well
    Global facies changes
    Unexpected posterior structures

--------------------------------------------------------------------
DIAGNOSIS WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Experiment 1

    Assimilate one measurement only.

Compute:

    delta = posterior - prior

Inspect:

    Mean update per latent variable

Question:

    Are all latent variables moving?

--------------------------------------------------

Experiment 2

    Compare:

        Prior geology
        Posterior geology

Question:

    Is change concentrated near measurement?

OR

    Is change spread across the entire model?

--------------------------------------------------

Experiment 3

    Plot:

        posterior_earth
            -
        prior_earth

Question:

    Do large changes occur far away from the
    measurement location?

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSSIBLE SOLUTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Short-term

    Accept as limitation.
    Continue validating workflow.

Medium-term

    Localize updates around well trajectory.

Long-term

    Implement localization inside PET.
    Update only cells influenced by measurement.

Priority:

    VERY HIGH

ISSUE 2
VERTICAL DOI MISMATCH
====================================================================

DESCRIPTION

Estimated GeoSphere sensitivity:

    ~40 m above receiver
    ~40 m below receiver

Approximate DOI:

    ~80 m total vertical extent

DISTINGUISH model:

    64 rows
    0.5 m per row

Total vertical size:

    32 m

Therefore:

    Real measurement sees geology outside the
    modeled domain.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The inversion tries to explain:

    80 m of physics

using only:

    32 m of geology

Potential consequences:

    Over-correction
    Artificial reservoir creation
    Non-physical facies patches
    Pink hallucinations

--------------------------------------------------------------------
DIAGNOSIS WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Compare:

    Synthetic assimilation

vs

    Real assimilation

Question:

    Do pink patches become significantly worse
    when using real data?

If yes:

    DOI mismatch becomes a strong suspect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSSIBLE SOLUTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Short-term

    Accept limitation.

Medium-term

    Center model vertically around trajectory.

Long-term

    Retrain GAN and proxy on larger vertical domain.

Priority:

    HIGH
